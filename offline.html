Offline mode

The installed Texas Health Access Navigator OS can continue to provide bundled educational content, calculators, local IndexedDB records, saved projects/plans and exports after its application shell has been cached.

Same-device collaboration: BroadcastChannel-based room traffic can continue between compatible tabs/windows on this device. Internet peer discovery: Trystero/Nostr/MQTT/BitTorrent/IPFS requires connectivity. Existing WebRTC peers may also disappear when the network is lost.

Live official directories, current eligibility systems, environmental feeds and first-time WebLLM package/model downloads require network access. Previously cached WebLLM models may work offline depending on browser cache state; the app does not guarantee this.

Return to the application. If the app shell has not been cached on this device yet, reconnect once and reload it.
